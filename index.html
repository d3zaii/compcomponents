06 / THE BIG PICTURE

Hardware gives AI
a place to run.

Your keyboard creates input. The CPU coordinates instructions. RAM holds active information. Storage keeps data. GPUs accelerate parallel workloads. The monitor shows the result. AI is the layer that can turn those computing resources into systems that learn patterns, generate content and help people solve problems.
